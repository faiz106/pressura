Pressura Support

We're happy to help. Email fziosapps@gmail.com and we aim to reply within 24 to 48 hours. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and a short description of the problem. Please don't send your readings. We don't need them to help you.

Subscriptions and refunds

  • Cancel: open the iPhone Settings app > [your name] > Subscriptions > Pressura.
  • Refund: Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • New phone or reinstalled the app? Open Pressura and tap Restore at the bottom of the subscription screen, or Settings > Subscription > Restore purchases.

My readings

  • Where are they kept? On your iPhone, inside the app. They are included in your iPhone or iCloud backup.
  • Moved to a new phone? Restore your iPhone from a backup. If you saved readings to Apple Health, you can also use Settings > Apple Health > Add readings from Health.
  • Sharing with my doctor: go to the Report tab to make a PDF, or tap Export a spreadsheet (CSV).

Reading a photo of my monitor

  • Hold the phone straight in front of the display, fill the frame and avoid glare from lights or windows.
  • Always check the numbers before saving. You can fix any number on the keypad.
  • To try it without a monitor, use Settings > Try a sample photo.

Why does my reading say "High-normal" or "Stage 1"?

Pressura names readings using a published guideline:

  • United States and Taiwan: American Heart Association. 130/80 and above counts as high.
  • Elsewhere: European Society of Hypertension. 140/90 and above counts as high, and 130–139 / 85–89 is "high-normal".

The app picks the one for your region. You can switch in Settings > Blood pressure categories. Your doctor may set a different target for you; set it under Settings > Your doctor's target.

Important

Pressura is a diary. It does not measure blood pressure, is not a medical device and does not diagnose. If you feel unwell or a reading is very high, contact your doctor or emergency services.
