Pressura Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Pressura ("the app") is a blood pressure diary for iPhone made by Muhammad Faiz Masroor ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your blood pressure readings stay on your iPhone. We never see them, sell them or use them for advertising.

1. Your readings and health information

  • The readings you log (blood pressure, pulse, time, arm, position, tags and notes) are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup.
  • If you turn on Apple Health, the app saves your readings to Apple Health and can read blood pressure and heart rate from it, so readings from other devices appear in your diary. You can change this at any time in the Health app or in the app's Settings.
  • Data from Apple Health is used only to show and save your readings inside the app. It is never sent to our server or to analytics, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold or shared with anyone.
  • Your answers to the setup questions (for example, why you track or whether you take medication), your doctor's target and the patient name for the PDF are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Your readings leave your iPhone only when you choose to share them, for example by sending the doctor PDF or the CSV file from the share sheet.

2. Reading a photo of your monitor

When you use "Scan monitor", the photo of your monitor's display is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which asks Google's Gemini AI model to read the numbers and sends them back to you to check. We do not store the photo, and it is not linked to your readings. To prevent abuse, our server keeps a daily count of scans per app install and per network address. The count uses an anonymous app ID and a one-way hash of your IP address, and it contains no photos, readings or personal details.

3. Analytics and crash reports

To understand which features are used and to fix bugs, the app sends anonymous usage events and error reports to PostHog, our analytics provider. Examples: "a reading was logged", "the PDF was made", "the paywall was shown". These events use a random ID created by the app. They never contain your readings, notes, names, onboarding answers or anything from Apple Health. We do not record your screen.

4. Subscriptions

Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active, using an anonymous ID. If you came to the app from an Apple Search Ads ad, Apple provides an attribution token so we can tell which ads lead to installs. It does not identify you.

5. What we don't do

  • No advertising in the app.
  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • No tracking you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, login or email address is required.

6. Service providers

  • Apple (App Store payments, Apple Health, iCloud backup)
  • Google Cloud and Google Gemini (our server and reading monitor photos)
  • RevenueCat (subscription status)
  • PostHog (anonymous analytics and error reports)

Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

7. Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app removes all readings stored in the app. Readings you saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.
  • To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app's anonymous ID, or for any privacy question, email fziosapps@gmail.com. If you live in the EU, UK or another region with data protection rights, you may ask for access to, correction of or deletion of data about you.

8. Children

Pressura is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Medical disclaimer

Pressura is a diary for the readings from your own monitor. It does not measure blood pressure, is not a medical device and does not diagnose or give treatment advice. Always follow your doctor's advice.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
